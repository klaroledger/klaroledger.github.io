Questions, answered

How a private, offline money tracker actually works.

Can I track my expenses without linking a bank account?

Yes — no banking login ever goes into the app. Add an expense in a couple of taps, or switch on capture and Klaro reads your bank's payment alerts on your device and turns them into transactions for you — no bank connection, nothing uploaded. Your credentials stay with your bank, and Klaro never needs read-access to your accounts.

Does it work offline?

Yes — Klaro is offline-first. Adding transactions, budgets, goals, and every chart all run on your phone, with no internet connection and no server round-trip. It's just as fast on a plane or with no signal.

Where is my financial data stored?

On your device only, in an encrypted database — there's no account and no cloud copy by default. If you turn on backup, it goes to your own private Google Drive folder, encrypted so only you can open it.

What's the difference between manual and automatic expense tracking?

Automatic apps link to your bank and pull your transactions from their servers — convenient, but it means handing over your banking login and your spending data. Klaro does it differently: log an expense yourself in a couple of taps, or let Klaro read your bank's payment alerts on your device and turn them into transactions for you. Either way, no banking login goes into the app, and nothing about your accounts is ever uploaded.

How does Klaro Ledger make money?

A simple subscription — US$1.99/month or US$17.99/year, with a 14-day free trial. That's the whole model: no ads, no trackers, and nothing about your spending is ever sold or shared. You pay for the app, so your data doesn't have to — which is why it can stay on your device.
